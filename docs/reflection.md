## An important review
A review quite early on which caused a lot of discussion was based on the structure of the project, which 
successively after a lot of back and forth made sure everyone knew which files we were working on.

## A difficult problem
Preventing reoccurring conflicts, as all parts are quite tightly knit, plus that each sprint mostly focused on 
many parts connected to one thing. The short and honest answer was that this was resolved by talking it out outside of PRs.

## What went well & what could improve
Because of correct priorities, the project had (end)points that worked quite early on from the start, and since the database was
setup, the essentials were already in place. Therefore, the remaining pieces (API key authentication and connection to the external API) didn't need any waiting on necessary parts. Things were developed in quite a good order.

Something that could be improved is either to be a little more flexible about the structure from the start, or 
break down how the big files could be broken down into smaller parts in cases where conflicts will become too frequent.
